AudiaHybrid ECE Instrumentation System

Analog speech signal processing platform with dual 433 MHz RF and 940 nm optical infrared transmission channels. Built from discrete analog components with zero digital DSP in the audio path.

As-Built Prototype Specifications
  • Pre-Amp: LM741 inverting configuration (Av ≈ 20–40 dB)
  • Speech Filter: Sallen-Key RC Low-Pass (fc ≈ 3.39 kHz)
  • Optical Tx: BC547 NPN modulated emitter (TSAL6100 940 nm)
  • Optical Rx: Silicon phototransistor + BC547 active pre-gain
  • Audio Power Amp: PAM8403 Class-D stereo / mono 3W output
